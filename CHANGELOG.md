## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

First official release.

- **Platforms and screens:** LINE (Official Account chat, Flex messages, rich messages, all 12 official rich-menu layouts plus tab style, lock-screen notifications), Facebook (feed ad, carousel, Stories/Reels, facebook.com home feed) and TikTok (For You in-feed ad, Spark Ad, product card, tiktok.com), on iPhone, Android and desktop.
- **Looks like the real thing:** phone frames built to the real iPhone 17 Pro and Galaxy S26 sizes; app screens, status bars, lock screens and app launch screens measured on real 2026 screenshots; the brands' own logos and app icons (copied, never redrawn).
- **Real photos:** your brand's photos, or real photos Claude finds online (Cowork and Claude Code), with every source logged.
- **Guided brief:** Claude asks only what it needs, helps plan the storyboard, and shows a picture of every rich-menu layout.
- **Output:** transparent high-res PNGs plus an overview board, in a phone frame or **screen only** (real screenshot size); one self-contained HTML file when images can't be rendered.
- **Walk-through videos:** several screens on one phone with taps, app launch screens and messages arriving, as MP4, 9:16, 16:9 or transparent `.mov`.
- **Update check:** in Claude Code and Cowork, Claude tells you when a newer version is out.
